Why I built Someday

I'm 56, and like a lot of people my age I started asking the obvious question: can I actually afford to retire — and when?

Every tool I tried either wanted my email and a phone call, buried the answer in jargon, or quietly skipped the bits that matter in the UK — the tax-free cash, the way tax works across ISAs and pensions, the State Pension, planning as a couple. None of them let me see the working.

So I did what I always do when I don't understand something: I built a spreadsheet.

That spreadsheet grew. It modelled my pension drawdown, the tax year by year, the bridge to State Pension age, what happened if markets had a bad decade. For the first time I could see a straight answer to a straight question — and change one number and watch it move.

Friends started asking for a copy. That's when it hit me: everyone deserves this, not just people who like spreadsheets. So I turned my model into Someday — the same maths, but as something anyone can use in a couple of minutes, that keeps your numbers on your own device, and that shows its working if you want to look.

It's free to use. If it helps you the way it helped me, you can chip in whatever you think it's worth — that's what funds the next idea.

— StuartFounder, Someday · built in the UK
