3. Privacy

Your data stays on your device

RetireLens is designed to be private by default. The figures you enter — ages, pension values, incomes, names — are processed entirely within your browser and stored locally on your own device (using your browser's local storage). We do not collect, transmit, receive or store your planning data on any server.

What we don't do

  • We do not run advertising or third-party tracking within the planner.
  • We do not sell or share your information — we don't have it to begin with.
  • We do not require an account or personal details to use the planner.

Hosting & server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, the host may automatically record standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in server access logs for security and reliability. This is handled by the host and is separate from your planning data, which stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. The fonts and charting library are served from this same site — the planner makes no requests to third-party servers.

Payments

If you purchase access, payment is handled by a third-party provider (such as Stripe). Any personal or payment information you give them is governed by their privacy policy, not ours. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, not your card details.

Clearing your data

You can remove everything stored locally at any time using the “Reset & Start Over” button in the planner, or by clearing your browser's site data.
